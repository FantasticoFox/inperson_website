Welcome to InPerson.Venture

At InPerson.Venture, we're dedicated to driving innovation, fostering growth, and empowering visionary entrepreneurs to transform industries. Our approach

We believe in the power of collaboration, expertise, and strategic guidance to unlock the full potential of bold ideas. Our seasoned team of investors, industry veterans, and advisors work hand-in-hand with founders to provide not just capital, but also invaluable insights, network access, and operational support.

What sets us apart

  • Sector expertise: From technology and healthcare to consumer goods and beyond, our team's deep domain knowledge enables us to identify disruptive opportunities and navigate complex markets with confidence.
  • Flexible capital: Whether it's seed funding, early-stage investment, or growth capital, we tailor our financing solutions to meet the unique needs of each startup, fueling their growth at every stage of development.
  • Global network: With a broad network spanning across industries and geographies, we connect our portfolio companies with strategic partners, customers, and talent to fuel their expansion and scale globally.
  • Long-term partnership: We're committed to building enduring relationships based on trust, transparency, and mutual success. Beyond financial backing, we're dedicated to being a trusted advisor and ally on the entrepreneurial journey.
