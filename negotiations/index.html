Negotiations & mediation

We help you negotiate effectively and confidently with suppliers, customers, partners and other stakeholders, and resolve the conflicts that arise during or after a negotiation.

Preparation

We analyze the strengths and weaknesses of your position and the interests and needs of the other party, then develop a negotiation strategy and plan tailored to your circumstances.

Guidance at the table

We guide and coach you through the process: communicating effectively, building rapport and finding common ground. The aim is an outcome that meets the needs of all parties while protecting your interests.

Mediation

When conflicts or disputes arise during or after a negotiation, we help the parties resolve them.

Lasting capability

Effective negotiation combines strategic thinking, communication skills and emotional intelligence. We work with you to develop all three.
